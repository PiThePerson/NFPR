WARD 01 · PHYSICAL THERAPY

Move again, measured every step

In the PT room, a single webcam becomes a tireless second therapist. NeuronFRAMES watches 13 body points in real time, guides each repetition with a live pose overlay, and turns prescribed exercise into games patients actually want to finish.

For the patient

  • Therapy that feels like play, not a chore
  • Clear, instant feedback on every move
  • Keep going at home, on any browser

For the care team

  • Every rep and form score captured automatically
  • Objective progress notes, no manual tallying
  • Guide more patients in the same hours
EPT Enhanced PT · live pose tracking GPT Gamified PT · motion games

How a session runs

  1. The therapist sets today's exercises and target range of motion.
  2. The patient follows a live on-screen guide while a single webcam tracks 13 body points in real time.
  3. Reps, range, and form score are captured automatically and saved to the chart.

In daily clinical use at King Chulalongkorn Memorial Hospital — part of 250+ patients treated every month.

WARD 02 · OCCUPATIONAL THERAPY

The small daily wins, made measurable

Buttoning a shirt. Holding a spoon. Gripping a rail. In the OT room, low-cost grip sensors feed live force data into therapy games, so hand and strength rehab becomes something a patient can see improving, session by session.

For the patient

  • Hands-on games that reward real effort
  • Feel progress in everyday tasks again
  • Affordable hardware, nothing intimidating

For the care team

  • Live grip force, logged automatically
  • Strength tracked objectively over time
  • Data to tune each plan with confidence
ITS Interactive Therapeutic System · Arduino + FSR grip sensors

How a session runs

  1. The patient grips low-cost force sensors wired into therapy games.
  2. Squeeze, hold, and release drive the game while live grip force is read.
  3. Strength and endurance are logged so progress shows session by session.

Hand & fine-motor rehab that turns invisible effort into a number the whole care team can follow.

See it on the platform
WARD 03 · SPEECH THERAPY

The first clear word, then the next

In the speech room, in-browser speech recognition coaches pronunciation, vocabulary, and communication, scoring every attempt. Patients can practise at any hour, and every word that comes back is a word recorded.

For the patient

  • Practise any time, without waiting for a slot
  • Gentle, scored feedback on every attempt
  • Hear the progress, word by word

For the care team

  • Accuracy scores tracked automatically
  • Scale practice beyond the clinic hour
  • Spot what to focus the next session on
SIT Speech Improvement Therapy · Web Speech API · pronunciation & vocabulary drills

How a session runs

  1. The patient is prompted with a word, sound, or phrase to practise.
  2. In-browser speech recognition listens and scores each attempt instantly.
  3. Accuracy is tracked over time, so the therapist sees exactly what to focus on next.

Practice no longer has to wait for the clinic hour — every word that comes back is a word recorded.
